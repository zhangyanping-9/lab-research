## 1. Cornell CNF

**Facility:** Cornell NanoScale Science & Technology Facility (CNF)  
**Website:** https://www.cnf.cornell.edu  
**NNCI Node:** Yes (National Nanotechnology Coordinated Infrastructure)  
**Role in Semiconductor Research:** Lead institution for **SUPREME Center** (JUMP 2.0)

### Major Research Directions

#### A. Topological Semimetals for Next-Generation Interconnects (SUPREME Center)

**Lifecycle:** `emerging` — Pre-commercial, lab-scale validation

**Description:** Research led by **Prof. Judy J. Cha** (MSE, Cornell) investigates topological semimetals (TSMs) — particularly Weyl semimetal NbAs (niobium arsenide) — as replacements for copper interconnects in advanced CMOS nodes. Below ~40 nm, Cu interconnects suffer from high resistance, RC delay, and electromigration. TSMs offer topologically protected surface states with low resistivity at reduced dimensions.

**Key Innovations:**
- **Surface-dominant transport:** NbAs nanowires exhibit decreasing resistivity as dimensions shrink (inverse of Cu behavior)
- **Thermomechanical nanomolding (TMNM):** High-throughput fabrication of single-crystal TSM nanowires with controlled diameters down to 10 nm (5x faster than previous methods)
- At 40 nm diameter, NbAs achieved **9.7 μΩ·cm** — better than ruthenium at same scale
- RIE etching of NbAs demonstrated at 5-7 nm/min under mild conditions

**Principal Investigators:**
- Judy J. Cha (Rich and Betty Tsai Ph.D. '81 Professor, MSE, Cornell) — Topological materials, SUPREME PI
- Yeryun Cheon (Postdoc / researcher, Cha Group)
- Mehrdad T. Kiani (Researcher, Cha Group)

**Key Publications (2025):**
- M.T. Kiani & J.J. Cha, "Shrinking interconnects beyond copper," *Science* **390**(6773), Nov 2025 — acknowledges SUPREME Center support
- Kiani, Tyner, Jog, Chen, Rondinelli, & Cha, "Searching for materials for next-generation on-chip interconnects," *Newton* **1**(5), July 2025 — proposes integrated co-design framework for high-throughput TSM discovery
- Cheon, Kiani, Cha et al., "Surface-dominant transport in Weyl semimetal NbAs nanowires for next-generation interconnects" — demonstrates NbAs outperforms Cu at nanoscale

**Funding:**
- **SUPREME Center** — a JUMP 2.0 center co-sponsored by **SRC** and **DARPA**
- **NSF** — CNF REU program support
- Target: Resistivity < 13 μΩ·cm at half-pitch w ≤ 8 nm for BEOL viability

#### B. SUPREME Center — Other Research Thrusts

**Lifecycle:** `growing`

- **Spintronics and memory** — Dan Ralph (Cornell), Luqiao Liu (MIT)
- **Wide-bandgap semiconductors** — Grace Xing (Cornell)
- **ML/AI-accelerated materials discovery** — multiple PIs
- **2025 SUPREME e-Workshops** on ML-AI processes, spintronics, heterogeneous integration

### Major Research Directions

#### A. Mid-Infrared Integrated Photonics (Capasso Group)

**Lifecycle:** `growing` — Multiple Nature/Science-level breakthroughs in 2025

**Description:** The Federico Capasso group has had a transformative year in mid-infrared integrated photonics, with breakthrough demonstrations of on-chip frequency combs and soliton lasers fabricated at Harvard CNS.

**Key 2025 Breakthroughs:**

1. **Driven Bright Solitons on Mid-Infrared Laser Chip**
   - *Nature* **641**, 83–89 (April 2025)
   - First monolithic chip-scale, picosecond mid-infrared laser pulse generator with no external components
   - Combines quantum cascade laser (QCL) with nonlinear integrated photonics
   - Uses ring resonator architecture with Kerr microresonator-inspired soliton generation
   - Center wavelength: 8.3 µm, ~1 ps pulse duration at GHz repetition rates
   - Applications: environmental monitoring (CO₂, methane), medical imaging, industrial process control
   - **Funding:** NSF and DoD

2. **High-Power Quantum Walk Frequency Combs**
   - arXiv preprint, Feb 2025
   - Dry-etched racetrack QCL with resonant RF injection
   - >100 mW continuous-wave at room temperature (7.9 µm), >30 cm⁻¹ bandwidth, >10 GHz modulation
   - Paves way for fully integrated dual-comb spectrometers on a chip

3. **Widely Tunable Semiconductor Ring Laser Array**
   - *Optica*, July 2025
   - Multiple ring QCLs of slightly different sizes connected to shared waveguide
   - Smooth, wide-range tuning without moving parts — potential replacement for DFB and external cavity lasers

**Principal Investigator:**
- Federico Capasso (Robert L. Wallace Professor of Applied Physics, Harvard SEAS) — QCL pioneer, nanophotonics

#### B. Metalens & Metasurface Technologies (Capasso Group)

**Lifecycle:** `mature` — Approaching commercialization; 100mm diameter demonstrated

**2024–2025 Highlights:**
- **All-glass 100 mm diameter visible metalens** for astronomy (18.7 billion nanostructures) — *ACS Nano*, Jan 2024
- **Free-standing bilayer metasurfaces** in visible — *Nature Communications*, April 2025 (first demonstration)
- **Single-shot Mueller matrix imaging** using two metasurfaces — *Nature Photonics*, 2024
- **Flat 40° FOV polarization imager** (6×3 mm monolithic glass) — *Optica*, June 2025
- Shape optimization, metasurface-controlled microcavities, fiber coupling metasurfaces

**Funding:** NSF, DoD, private

#### C. CNS Facility — Research Infrastructure

**Key Equipment:**
- **Lithography:** Elionix ELS-F125 (125 keV, 5 nm resolution), Elionix ELS-7000, Raith-150, Heidelberg MLA-150 maskless, Nanoscribe 3D laser printing, i-line stepper
- **Deposition:** PECVD, LPCVD, ALD, e-beam evaporators, sputterers
- **Etching:** Oxford Cobra ICP-RIE, SPTS Rapier DRIE, Plasma-Therm ICP-RIE, Ion Beam Etcher, XeF₂ etcher

#### A. Post-CMOS Nanowire Computing Fabrics (Moritz Group)

**Lifecycle:** `maturing` — From concept toward demonstration

**Principal Investigator:** Csaba Andras Moritz (Professor, ECE, UMass Amherst)

**Key Projects:**
1. **Skybridge** — Vertical nanowire-based 3D integrated circuits beyond CMOS addressing circuit, connectivity, and thermal challenges in an integrated approach
2. **Physically-Equivalent Architectures for Bayesian Reasoning** — Non-volatile magneto-electric computational paradigm; up to 5 orders of magnitude improvement vs. state-of-the-art microprocessors
3. **NASIC / N3ASIC / NWRAM** — Nanowire-based computing fabrics for logic and memory
4. **Spin Wave Functions (SPWF)** — Ultra-low-power computation using electron spin wave superposition
5. **Graphene Nanoribbon-CMOS Multistate Memory (GNTRAM)** — Multistate memory using GNR crossbars exhibiting NDR

**Approach:** Cross-layer exploration (devices → circuits → architecture → manufacturing), experimental cleanroom work + theoretical

#### B. Protein Nanowire Bioelectronics (Yao Group)

**Lifecycle:** `emerging`

**Principal Investigator:** Jun Yao (Professor, ECE)

- Artificial neurons using protein nanowires from *Geobacter sulfurreducens* — operate at ~0.1V
- Memristor architecture, connected to heart-tissue cells
- Applications: brain-machine interfaces, prosthetics, low-power bioelectronics

**Air-Gen Technology:**
- Harvests electricity from ambient humidity
- 2025 NSF ASCENT grant for scaling into integrated electronics
- PhD student Xiaomeng Liu: $61K Link Foundation Energy Fellowship for Air-Gen

#### C. Nanoimprint Lithography — New Equipment

**Lifecycle:** `emerging`

- EVG 7200 Nanoimprint Lithography Tool arriving June 2025, operational late summer/fall 2025
- Part of $5M award for Advanced Optics Fabrication and Characterization Facility (open-access)

#### D. Graphene Bioelectronics (Kireev Group)

- Reduced graphene oxide (rGO) for non-genetic neural stimulation — GraMOS platform
- *Nature Communications*, Sept 2025
- Stem-cell-derived neuron maturation and brain organoid development

#### E. Institute for Hierarchical Manufacturing Upgrades

- Senior Cleanroom Engineer position posted (2024–2025)
- Equipment: evaporators, sputterers, plasma etchers (including deep Si etch), ALD, wafer bonders, mask aligners
