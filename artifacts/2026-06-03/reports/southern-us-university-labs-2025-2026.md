## 1. UT Austin MRC/TIE (utaustin-mrc)

### Facility Overview
The University of Texas at Austin's **Microelectronics Research Center (MRC)** houses a 12,000 sq. ft. shared-use cleanroom (Class 100/Class 1000) supporting R&D in electronic, photonic, optoelectronic, spintronic, and MEMS devices. The **Texas Institute for Electronics (TIE)** is the larger organizational umbrella driving the DARPA NGMM center.

**Leadership:**
- MRC Faculty Director: **Dr. Jean Anne Incorvia** (Associate Prof., ECE)
- MRC Managing Director: **Dr. Roberto Panepucci** (formerly Cornell NanoScale)
- TIE CEO: **Dwayne LaBrake**

### Key Research Directions

| Direction | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **3D Heterogeneous Integration (3DHI)** | Core NGMM focus -- stacking separately manufactured components (Si + GaN, SiC, photonics) within a single package |
| **Mixed-Material 3DHI** | Si + III-V, GaN, SiC, photonic integration; up to ~100x performance gains over 2D |
| **AI-Driven Process Modeling** | Via Austin startup Sandbox Semiconductor for process optimization |
| **Holographic Metasurface Nano-Lithography (HMNL)** | 3D printing method announced Dec 2025 for chip packaging |
| **Spintronics & MEMS** | Traditional MRC research areas |

### Major Projects & Funding

| Project | Funding | Timeline | Description |
|---------|---------|----------|-------------|
| **NGMM TIE Center (TNC)** | $840M (DARPA) + $552M (Texas) = ~$1.4B | 5 years (Phase 1: Jul 2024; Phase 2 start 2026-27) | First national open-access 3DHI center; equipment install by Q1 2026 |
| **3DHI Exemplar Demonstrators** | Part of NGMM | 2024-2029 | Phased-array radar, IR focal plane array, compact power converter |
| **Texas CHIPS Act Allocation** | ~$440M | 2024-2030 | State funding for TIE infrastructure |

### Key Publications & Milestones
- **Dec 2025**: Holographic Metasurface Nano-Lithography (HMNL) announced
- **Oct 2025**: Inaugural NGMM Summit in Austin
- **May 2026**: "Future of Microchips" -- Texas described as "capitol of the U.S. semiconductor resurgence"
- **May 2026**: PhD student Ian Anderson, 2nd Place Paper at IEEE Microwave Acoustics & Mechanics Conference

### Ecosystem

- UT Dallas, Texas Tech for workforce development
- PDK/ADK development underway

## Cross-Cutting Themes (2025-2026)

1. **3D Heterogeneous Integration (3DHI)** -- Dominant theme at UT Austin TIE ($1.4B NGMM), Georgia Tech PRC ($5.3M DARPA HAPPI, GlobalFoundries Chair). These two institutions are the Southern US leaders in 3DHI.

2. **Wide Bandgap Semiconductors (SiC, GaN, Diamond, Ga2O3)** -- NC State CLAWS Hub ($39.4M+), University of Arkansas MUSiC ($46.5M), UF NRF (III-N), Vanderbilt VINSE (Mona Ebrish group). The CLAWS Hub and MUSiC form a powerful WBG corridor in the Southeast.

3. **Photonic AI & Optical Computing** -- UF (Volker Sorger -- ISS-tested photonic AI chips, 100x efficiency), Georgia Tech (Ali Adibi -- DARPA HAPPI 3D optical routing), Vanderbilt (Jason Valentine -- meta-optics for edge computing).

4. **Texas Semiconductor Megaprojects** -- UT Austin TIE ($1.4B NGMM) + Texas A&M TSI ($226M) + Texas CHIPS Act ($948M total). Texas is positioning itself as a national semiconductor manufacturing R&D hub.

5. **Quantum Technologies** -- UMD ($1B Capital of Quantum, IonQ, Microsoft, DARPA), JHU APL (quantum sensing), Vanderbilt (nonlinear optics), NC State (quantum semiconductor workshop).

6. **Workforce Development** -- Every lab has workforce/training programs: UF/FAMU HBCU program, NC State CLAWS REU, Virginia Tech certificate program, Texas A&M WaVe-Chip, UMD $1.2M NSF middle school program, Vanderbilt REU (15th year).

7. **Defense-Focused Funding** -- DARPA (NGMM, HAPPI, BTO), DoD (CLAWS Hub, JHU APL), ARPA-H, NASA drive the majority of large-scale semiconductor R&D at these institutions.
