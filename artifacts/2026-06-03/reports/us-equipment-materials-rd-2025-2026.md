### EUV Underlayer R&D (SPIE 2025, Vol. 13428)
- **MOR sensitivity improvement**: ~20% dose reduction for Metal Oxide Resists using functional underlayers; tested on NXE3400B at 14nm HP line-space
- **Functional underlayers/primers**: Reduced dose and defectivity for dense contact hole patterning (CAR) and dense L/S pitch 24nm (MOR)

- **Hybrid bonding enablement** (Dr. Andrea Chacko): Materials for direct die-to-wafer and collective die-to-wafer hybrid bonding
